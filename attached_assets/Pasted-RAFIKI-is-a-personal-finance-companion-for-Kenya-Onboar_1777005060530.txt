RAFIKI is a personal finance companion for Kenya. Onboarding is complete 
and working — the app can read a user's bank statements, understand their 
spending, ask about unknowns, and build a priority stack. That foundation 
is solid.

Now we need to build what RAFIKI actually is. The onboarding was the 
setup. This is the product.

---

THE CORE PROMISE WE HAVEN'T BUILT YET

A user should be able to open RAFIKI and ask "can I afford to go out for 
nyama choma this Friday, about 3k?" and get a specific, honest answer. 
Not a generic budget tip. Not a percentage. A real answer: yes you can 
and here's what you'll have left, or no you can't because rent lands in 
4 days and here's exactly what the shortfall would be.

That is the product. Everything else — the goals screen, the activity log, 
the standing instructions — is presentation on top of that core capability.

Build the core first. Then surface it through the screens.

---

THE BRAIN BEHIND EVERY ANSWER

Every financial answer RAFIKI gives must come from deterministic logic, 
not from the AI guessing. The AI's job is to understand what the user 
is asking and explain the answer in a warm, conversational way. The logic's 
job is to compute the actual numbers. These two things must never be mixed.

When a user asks about spending, the logic must know: what is genuinely 
available after all upcoming commitments are accounted for, what is already 
spoken for before the next salary, and whether a proposed spend would put 
any obligation at risk. The AI reads those results and speaks them back 
in RAFIKI's voice.

The priority cascade the user set up during onboarding is the rulebook. 
Every financial decision runs through it: survival obligations first, 
social commitments second, savings and growth third, lifestyle last.

---

THE MOMENT MONEY ARRIVES

When salary lands, RAFIKI should spring into action before the user 
spends a shilling. It reads the priority stack, works out where every 
shilling should go, and presents the user with a clean summary: here 
is how I have arranged your money, does this look right? The user 
confirms or adjusts. This is the most powerful moment in the product 
and it should feel like a relief, not a chore.

---

THE RED ALERT

If a user wants to spend money that would put a survival obligation at 
risk, RAFIKI does not just say no. It explains exactly what is at risk, 
how much the shortfall would be, and whether there is a way to free up 
the money by deferring a lower-priority commitment. It offers a specific 
path forward, not a wall.

This should feel like a calm, honest friend telling you something 
important — not a warning alarm.

---

THE CONVERSATION INTERFACE

The conversation screen is the product. This is where the user lives. 
It should feel like messaging someone who knows your finances deeply 
and cares about getting it right.

RAFIKI speaks first sometimes. When the user opens the app and their 
salary has just arrived, RAFIKI says so. When a standing instruction 
fired overnight, RAFIKI mentions it. When the user is close to the 
edge of a budget category, RAFIKI brings it up before the user asks.

When RAFIKI proposes an action — sending money, saving an amount, 
allocating a transfer — it shows a clear approval card inside the 
conversation. The user confirms with one tap. RAFIKI never acts 
without that confirmation.

The conversation history persists. When the user opens the app 
tomorrow, they see the thread from today. RAFIKI remembers what 
was said.

---

THE SCREENS

The Figma designs for the following screens have been pasted into 
this session. Build them so that every number, every status, every 
piece of content comes from the actual financial data built during 
onboarding — not sample values.

Home: shows the user's genuinely available balance (not the raw 
account total — what they can actually spend without risking any 
obligation), a single health score with a one-line explanation, 
and one contextual nudge from RAFIKI. Everything on this screen 
answers the question "am I okay right now?" in under three seconds.

Conversation: the full message thread with RAFIKI's messages and 
the user's messages. Inline approval cards appear when RAFIKI is 
proposing an action. The input bar is always visible at the bottom.

Snapshot sheet: slides up from the home screen when the user wants 
more detail. Shows the health score with explanation, spending by 
category as horizontal bars with real KSh amounts, and progress 
on the top active goal.

Goals: a list of the user's savings goals with real progress. Each 
goal shows how far along it is, whether it is on track to hit the 
deadline, and how much is being contributed each week. Status is 
computed from actual progress against the target date — not a label 
someone set manually.

Activity log: every financial event in chronological order. Salary 
arrivals, transfers, savings contributions, standing instructions 
firing. Filterable by type. This is the audit trail — the user 
should be able to see everything RAFIKI has done on their behalf.

Standing instructions: the conditional automations the user has 
set up. Each one shows what it does and when, whether it is 
active or paused, and when it last fired. Paused ones show why 
they are paused. Adding a new one opens the conversation.

---

WHAT GOOD LOOKS LIKE

A user opens the app on the day their salary arrives. RAFIKI has 
already run the numbers. The home screen shows their available 
balance after all obligations are accounted for. They open the 
conversation and RAFIKI says: your salary arrived, here is how I 
have arranged it, want me to go ahead?

They confirm. Then they ask: can I take the family out this 
weekend, maybe 5,000 bob? RAFIKI runs the numbers and comes back: 
yes, you will have KSh 2,800 left in your lifestyle budget after. 
Or: that would put you below your buffer before rent — but your 
chama contribution is not due for 12 days and your salary arrives 
in 8, so you could defer it this cycle if you want.

That is the product working.
