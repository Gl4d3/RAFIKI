We are updating the RAFIKI onboarding pipeline based on testing with real 
financial data. We are describing what the real data looks like, what the 
system needs to understand about it, and what the pipeline must correctly 
produce. Build against these specifics, not assumptions.

---

WHAT THE REAL DATA LOOKS LIKE

We have tested with a real I&M Bank KSh current account statement 
(PAYGO-LCY account type). Here is what the actual data contains and 
what the parser must correctly handle:

Transaction narratives take these forms in I&M Bank PDFs:
  - "254728125443/MPESA Payment to 254728125443" — M-Pesa transfer to a 
    phone number. No name is given in the bank statement.
  - "CTS/EFT CR BO INDRA LIMITED" — an EFT credit, in this case a salary 
    deposit from an employer
  - "01192274659800/Rent Payment" — a paybill payment with a label
  - "00204980506150/" — a paybill with no label (appears as both 
    deposits and withdrawals in the same statement)
  - "CHANDARANA SUPER727393 AIROBI0327" — a card payment at a physical 
    merchant with location and date codes appended
  - "MICROSOFT*STORE MSBILL0302 103419PRCR5506" — an international 
    online subscription with billing reference
  - "Spotify Stockh0326 023803PRCR5506" — streaming subscription
  - "Google CLOUD F4QzK3 Dublin0326 114255PRCR5506" — cloud service
  - "PesaPal*Century Cinema Nairob0326 193419PRCR5506" — PesaPal payment
  - "Charge 01192274659800/Rent Payment" — bank charge on a transaction
  - "Excise Duty 01192274659800/Rent Payment" — government excise on a 
    transaction
  - "3695448/MPESA Payment to 3695448" — a till number, no merchant name

The parser must handle all of these formats. Bank charges and excise duty 
entries (rows with "Charge" or "Excise Duty" prefixes referencing another 
transaction) should be attached to their parent transaction as fees, not 
treated as independent transactions.

---

A CRITICAL PATTERN: THE SELF-TRANSFER LOOP

The paybill 00204980506150 appears in this statement as both incoming 
deposits and outgoing withdrawals. This is the user's own M-Pesa number — 
the user is transferring money between their bank account and their M-Pesa 
wallet in both directions. These transactions must be identified as internal 
transfers and excluded from spending analysis. They are not income and not 
expenses — they are liquidity movements between the user's own accounts.

The system must detect this pattern: a single counterparty identifier that 
appears on both sides of the ledger within the same statement period is 
almost certainly an internal transfer and should be flagged as such. The 
LLM should confirm this during enrichment using the flag_internal_transfer 
tool before the Accountant excludes it from category totals.

WHAT THE ANALYSIS MUST CORRECTLY PRODUCE FROM THIS STATEMENT

Salary detection:
The credit "CTS/EFT CR BO INDRA LIMITED" for KSh 59,335 on 24 March is 
the salary. The Accountant must identify EFT credits from consistent 
employer names as salary events. The employer name should be stored as 
a known entity.

Rent detection:
The debit to "01192274659800/Rent Payment" for KSh 18,000 on 25 March 
(the day after salary) is rent. The narrative explicitly says "Rent 
Payment". This should be auto-categorised as Housing & Rent, Tier 1, 
without needing to ask the user.

The large unknown withdrawal:
KSh 55,000 to paybill 00204980503550 on the same day as salary arrival. 
This is a large round-number withdrawal that is not a known paybill. 
It must be surfaced in gap-filling as a high-priority unknown — it is 
the single largest outflow and its category is unknown. The question 
to the user should reflect the amount, timing (same day as salary), 
and that it appears to be a one-time large transfer.

Groceries:
CHANDARANA appears three times (SUPER, FOODPLUS, NGARA branches) for 
a combined KSh 5,408. These are all the same supermarket chain and 
should be aggregated into a single "Food & Groceries" category total. 
The parser must normalise "CHANDARANA SUPER727393 AIROBI..." to 
"Chandarana" regardless of branch suffix.

Subscriptions:
Microsoft Store, Spotify, and Google Cloud should be auto-categorised. 
Microsoft and Spotify go under Lifestyle / Subscriptions (Tier 4). 
Google Cloud is ambiguous — could be work-related or personal. It 
should be flagged for gap-filling.

Entertainment:
PesaPal*Century Cinema three times on the same evening (26 March) for 
a combined KSh 1,960. These should be grouped as a single outing under 
Entertainment (Tier 4), not three separate transactions.

Recurring unknown person:
Phone number 254728125443 appears approximately 15 times across the 
month for a combined total over KSh 12,000. This is clearly a recurring 
person with a significant financial relationship. The gap-filling question 
for this number must be the first question asked, must show the full 
frequency and total, and must offer relationship options relevant to 
Kenyan context: family member, domestic worker, debt repayment, 
regular business payment, or other.

Secondary recurring person:
Phone number 254114085820 appears 3 times for a combined KSh 1,800. 
Also needs gap-filling but lower priority than the above.

---

THE BANK-TO-MPESA RELATIONSHIP

This is a key architectural understanding the system needs: for this 
user, M-Pesa is not a separate financial system — it is a cash 
distribution layer fed by the bank account. The bank statement shows 
money moving to M-Pesa (via the self-transfer paybill), and then 
M-Pesa statements would show how that cash was actually spent on 
smaller daily transactions (transport, food stalls, small sends).

The pipeline must be designed to accept both sources together and 
understand that they represent different layers of the same financial 
life. When both are uploaded:

- Bank statement provides: salary, rent, subscriptions, supermarket 
  card purchases, large transfers
- M-Pesa statement provides: daily transport, small food purchases, 
  named recipients for person-to-person transfers, airtime, data bundles

The Accountant must merge these into a single transaction set, 
deduplicate any amounts that appear in both (a bank withdrawal to M-Pesa 
and an M-Pesa receipt of the same amount on the same day are one event), 
and produce unified category totals.

---

MULTI-SOURCE UPLOAD REQUIREMENTS

The upload interface must accept:
- Multiple bank statement PDFs (different months, different accounts)
- M-Pesa statement PDFs or CSVs
- Pasted M-Pesa SMS text (for users who have SMS records but no 
  exported statement)
- Each uploaded source should be shown as a labelled card the user 
  can remove before analysis begins

More months of data produces better pattern detection. The UI should 
communicate this clearly. The minimum useful input is one month of 
bank statements or M-Pesa activity.

---

USER ANNOTATION STEP

Between upload and analysis, the user should be able to add a short 
free-text note giving RAFIKI context the statements cannot show. 
This note must be passed to the LLM as the highest-priority context 
during enrichment. Examples of what users write:
  - "The number 0728125443 is my mother"
  - "I transferred 55,000 to my savings account"
  - "My salary comes from Indra on the 24th of every month"

If the user explains a transaction in this note, the LLM must use 
that explanation and must not ask about it again in gap-filling.

---

GAP-FILLING QUESTION QUALITY

Gap-filling questions must be generated by the LLM and must be specific 
to the actual transaction data. Generic questions are not acceptable.

Good question for 254728125443:
"I noticed you sent money to the number 0728125443 about 15 times in 
March, totalling KSh 12,000+. Transfers were spread across the month 
with no fixed pattern. Who is this person?"

Bad question (do not do this):
"Who is this contact?"

For the KSh 55,000 withdrawal to an unknown paybill on salary day:
"On the same day your salary arrived, KSh 55,000 went to paybill 
00204980503550 — that's most of your salary in a single transfer. 
Is this a savings account, rent, an investment, or something else?"

---

WHAT SHOULD NOT CHANGE

The existing visual design is correct. The design tokens, screen layouts, 
and component styles from the current implementation must not be touched. 
Only the data pipeline and data binding change.

The category and tier structure is correct:
  Tier 1 — Survival: Housing & Rent, Food & Groceries, Transport, Utilities
  Tier 2 — Social Obligations: Family, Domestic support, Chama
  Tier 3 — Growth: Savings, Emergency buffer, Investments
  Tier 4 — Lifestyle: Entertainment, Subscriptions, Dining out

The priority stack shows categories with aggregated totals, not 
individual merchants or individual transactions.
